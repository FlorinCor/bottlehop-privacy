Delete your game data

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Bottle Hop has no player account or developer-hosted cloud save. Your best score and control and graphics preferences are stored on your device.

Remove local data on Android

  1. Open your device’s Settings and select Apps.
  2. Select Bottle Hop (or Bottle Hop v2 for the development build).
  3. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  4. Select Clear storage or Clear data and confirm.

Menu labels vary by device. This resets your saved best score and settings; clearing only the cache does not remove those preferences. Uninstalling the current Android app also removes its local app data.

Advertising information

Advertising is planned through Google AdMob and is not integrated in the current development build. For ad-supported releases, clearing game data does not automatically erase information already processed by Google. Use the choices and contact options in Google’s Privacy Policy and your device’s advertising-ID settings.

Support messages and privacy requests

Email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com with the subject “Bottle Hop data deletion” to ask about deleting support correspondence held by the developer. Mention the email address you used to contact support. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and any necessary legal retention obligations.
